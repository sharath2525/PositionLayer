# Stocks company marks

These unbadged PNG company marks were retrieved on 2026-09-29 from Financial Modeling Prep's public symbol image endpoint, `https://images.financialmodelingprep.com/symbol/{SYMBOL}.png`. They are presentation assets only, not market data or proof of endorsement. Original files are stored locally; no image-provider calls are made by visitors for these companies.

Symbols: AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, TSM, AVGO, META, JPM, LLY, WMT, V, ORCL, MA, XOM, COST, NFLX, MU, AMD, BRK-B, ASML, JNJ.

Other supported company vectors reuse the project's pinned Simple Icons marks. Remaining assets use the issuer-confirmed image in a circular viewport, with a symbol fallback on error. Brand rights remain with their respective owners.
